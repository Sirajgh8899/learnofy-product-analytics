# Insights

These conclusions are based only on the synthetic sample dataset in `data/sample/`.

- Average weekly active users in the sample dataset is 23.5.
- The `high_engagement` segment has the highest average study hours per student in the sample.
- `Espressolab` has the strongest sample promotion claim rate among cafes with generated promotion activity.
- Study-room activity and dashboard/rewards page views are the most frequent synthetic engagement signals in the feature usage table.

These are sample-data findings, not production Learnofy business results.
