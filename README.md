Learnofy is a two-sided platform designed for university students and campus cafés.

From a data analytics perspective, the project focuses on understanding student engagement, feature usage, café promotion performance, user feedback, and product adoption in order to support product and business decisions.

The project combines:

* Product Analytics
* User Research
* KPI Design
* Data Interpretation
* Dashboard Logic
* Business Intelligence
* User Segmentation
* AI-Assisted Product Development

Business Problem
<img width="1600" height="1125" alt="PHOTO-2026-05-02-21-36-53" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/59337eb4-4ce0-4f26-91eb-6d3e9b32fd54" />

Learnofy serves two different user groups:

1. University students who want better academic organization and study support.
2. Campus cafés that want more targeted and measurable ways to reach students.

The analytical challenge is to understand how both groups interact with the platform and determine which features and activities generate the most engagement and value.

Analytical Objectives

The main analytics questions behind the project include:

* Which student features generate the highest engagement?
* Which features are underused?
* How frequently do students interact with study-planning tools?
* Which user segments are most active?
* Which café promotions receive the highest engagement?
* What types of offers perform best?
* How does engagement change over time?
* Which behaviors may indicate stronger user retention?
* What improvements should be prioritized based on user feedback and usage patterns?

For early-stage analysis, qualitative interview feedback was organized into structured categories to identify recurring patterns and product priorities.

User Research Analysis

User interviews were conducted with students and café owners to understand their needs, frustrations, and preferences.

Feedback was grouped into common themes such as:

* Study planning
* Motivation
* Peer collaboration
* Course organization
* Café discovery
* Promotions
* Student engagement

Conflicting responses were analyzed by comparing:

* frequency of the issue;
* importance to users;
* feasibility;
* alignment with product goals.

This helped translate qualitative feedback into structured product decisions.

  Data Analysis Workflow

The analytics workflow for Learnofy follows a structured process:

1. Collect user and product data
2. Clean and organize the data
3. Validate data quality
4. Define business metrics and KPIs
5. Segment users
6. Analyze trends and patterns
7. Build dashboards
8. Interpret findings
9. Present recommendations
10. Measure the impact of changes
