# Analytics Questions

This portfolio analytics layer answers questions a junior data analyst, BI analyst, or product analyst might investigate for Learnofy.

## Student Engagement

- How many students are active each week?
- Which students study consistently versus occasionally?
- How much study time is generated by each engagement segment?
- Which product actions are associated with stronger engagement?

## Study Behavior

- What is the total and average study time per student?
- Which students have the longest active-day streaks?
- How do study sessions contribute to points and leaderboard rank?

## Feature Usage

- Which features are used most often in the sample data?
- How many unique students use dashboard, rewards, notes, study room, and promotions features?

## Cafe Promotion Performance

- Which cafes generate the most promotion views?
- Which promotions have the highest claim rate?
- How many claimed promotions are redeemed in the sample data?

## Retention

- Do students return in later weeks after their signup cohort week?
- How does cohort retention change over the first several weeks?

Retention is included only in the portfolio analytics layer because the synthetic sample dataset includes both `created_at` and daily activity dates.
