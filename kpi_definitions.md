# KPI Definitions

All KPI definitions below apply to the synthetic sample analytics dataset unless explicitly labeled as existing app logic.

## Weekly Active Users

Formula: count of distinct `student_id` values with `active_seconds > 0` during a calendar week.

Business meaning: measures how many students returned to study during the week.

Source: `synthetic_student_activity_daily.csv`.

## Total Study Hours

Formula: `SUM(active_seconds) / 3600`.

Business meaning: total learning activity generated by students.

Source: `synthetic_student_activity_daily.csv` and `synthetic_study_sessions.csv`.

## Average Study Minutes On Active Day

Formula: `AVG(active_seconds where active_seconds > 0) / 60`.

Business meaning: intensity of study behavior on days when students are active.

Source: `synthetic_student_activity_daily.csv`.

## Active Days

Formula: count of days where `active_seconds > 0`.

Business meaning: frequency of student engagement.

Source: `synthetic_student_activity_daily.csv`.

## Longest Streak

Formula: maximum number of consecutive dates where a student has `active_seconds > 0`.

Business meaning: consistency of study behavior.

Source: `synthetic_student_activity_daily.csv`.

## Total Points

Formula: `SUM(points)` by student.

Business meaning: gamified engagement score used for rewards and leaderboard ranking.

Source: `synthetic_points_transactions.csv`.

## Leaderboard Rank

Formula: rank students by `SUM(points)` descending.

Business meaning: identifies highest-engagement students in the gamification system.

Source: `synthetic_points_transactions.csv`.

## Promotion Views

Formula: count of rows in `synthetic_student_promotions.csv`.

Business meaning: number of student-promotion exposure records in the sample data.

Source: `synthetic_student_promotions.csv`.

## Promotion Claim Rate

Formula: `claimed promotions / viewed promotions`.

Business meaning: measures how compelling cafe offers are after student exposure.

Source: `synthetic_student_promotions.csv`.

## Promotion Redemption Rate

Formula: `redeemed promotions / claimed promotions`.

Business meaning: measures follow-through after students claim promotions.

Source: `synthetic_student_promotions.csv`.

## Feature Unique Users

Formula: count of distinct `student_id` by `feature_name` and `event_type`.

Business meaning: shows adoption of product areas such as dashboard, rewards, notes, study room, and promotions.

Source: `synthetic_feature_usage.csv`.

## Cohort Retention

Formula: retained users in week N after signup cohort week divided by total users in the signup cohort.

Business meaning: measures whether students come back after joining.

Source: `synthetic_students.csv` and `synthetic_student_activity_daily.csv`.
